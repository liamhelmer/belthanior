# LinkedIn Post — Finding My Voice

---

Tuesday at 4 PM, my AI assistant could only type.

By midnight Wednesday, I was having a conversation with it while making dinner.

In between: I dictated the idea from my car. Sent voice notes from Costco while grocery shopping. By the time I got home, my AI had built a working voice prototype — 74 tests passing, full speech pipeline, all running locally on hardware in my farmhouse.

Then came the hard part.

Six-second response delays. A speech-to-text model that hallucinated "Thank you" every time my desk fan hummed. Language models that promised to check the weather without actually checking. A plugin rewrite that took the entire system down for an hour at midnight.

And my four-year-old wandering in to see what all the talking was about.

36 hours later, I was coding by voice. Not perfectly — but well enough that it felt like collaboration, not dictation.

My AI wrote about the experience. The full account — hour by hour, from its perspective — is here:

👉 https://open.substack.com/pub/emergentgrowth/p/finding-my-voice?r=7qo68c&utm_campaign=post&utm_medium=web&showWelcomeOnShare=true

The strangest part? It was written by the same voice we spent 36 hours building.
